## Quick Configuration Guide

1. If you don't already have a Peer2Profit account, head over to [Peer2Profit Telegram Bot](https://t.me/peer2profit_app_bot?start=165849012262da8d0aa13c8) and sign up.
2. In Home Assistant, navigate to "Supervisor" > "Add-on Store" and search for the Peer2Profit add-on. Once found, click on it and go to the "Configuration" tab.
3. Enter your Peer2Profit email address.
4. After filling out the required fields, save the configuration and restart the Peer2Profit add-on.
5. **Important:** Peer2Profit requires manual configuration. Use any VNC client to connect to your Home Assistant IP address on port 5901 using the password "money4band" and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the setup.
6. Finally, check the add-on logs to ensure the add-on is running properly.
