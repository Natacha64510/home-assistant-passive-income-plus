## Quick Configuration Guide

1. If you don't already have a PacketShare account, head over to [packetshare.io](https://www.packetshare.io?code=4C4B12627AA97CEE) and sign up.
2. In Home Assistant, navigate to "Supervisor" > "Add-on Store" and search for the PacketShare add-on. Once found, click on it and go to the "Configuration" tab.
3. In your PacketShare account, you will need your email and password to link your device. It's recommended to avoid using special characters in your PacketShare password, as they may cause issues during the setup process.
4. After filling out the required fields, save the configuration and restart the PacketShare add-on.
5. Finally, check the add-on logs to ensure the add-on has successfully connected to your PacketShare account.
